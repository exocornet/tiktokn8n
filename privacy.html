Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

1. Overview

n8n TikTok Uploader is a personal automation tool. It does not operate as a public-facing service and does not collect personal data from other users.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell personal data from third parties.

The tool only uses:

  • TikTok OAuth tokens to authenticate the account owner's TikTok account
  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic scope) to identify the connected account
  • Local video files stored on the owner's server for upload purposes

3. How Data Is Used

OAuth tokens are used solely to upload videos to the account owner's TikTok profile. Video files are processed locally on the owner's server and transmitted directly to TikTok.

4. Data Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. Video content is sent only to TikTok via the official TikTok Content Posting API.

5. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are stored locally in the owner's n8n instance. The owner may revoke access at any time through TikTok account settings.

6. Your Rights

As the sole user, you may revoke TikTok app authorization at any time via TikTok Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner directly.
